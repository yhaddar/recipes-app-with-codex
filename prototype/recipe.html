Sunlit lemon pasta

Silky, bright, and generous enough for the people who always stay for dinner. The kind of recipe that becomes a little more yours every time.

AMBy Amelia Moreau · 12 recipes

Ingredients

  • 320g spaghetti
  • 2 unwaxed lemons
  • 80g parmesan, finely grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 handful fresh basil
  • Sea salt & black pepper
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
the rhythm

Instructions

  1. Boil the water.
    Salt generously and cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente.
  2. Wake up the lemon.
    Warm olive oil, garlic, chili, and zest in a wide pan until fragrant.
  3. Bring it together.
    Add pasta, a splash of cooking water, lemon juice, and parmesan. Toss until glossy.
  4. Finish with green.
    Fold through basil, taste for salt, and serve while the sauce is still shining.
