A few good rules.

This prototype is a visual demonstration, not a live service or binding agreement.

Share with care

In the final product, publish only recipes, images, and stories you have the right to share. Keep private information out of public content and respect the work of other cooks.

Prototype note

Buttons and forms demonstrate intended flows. They do not create accounts, save data, send emails, or process payments.
